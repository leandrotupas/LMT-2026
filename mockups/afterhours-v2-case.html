Advenient

From a generic visual identity to a distinct, forward-moving brand built for a growing digital marketing company.

Role
Lead brand designer
Year
2024
Scope
Identity and print

A mark that moves forward.

The client wanted the logo to symbolize growth. To make that idea simple and immediately recognizable, I used a caret (^)—a symbol commonly used to express exponents—as the foundation of the mark.

The brief

Advenient is a digital marketing company focused on helping businesses grow and giving talent space to develop their careers. The company had outgrown its original visual identity, which no longer reflected the energy or breadth of its work.

I personally led the project from client communication through design. The client wanted teal and orange because they felt this combination differentiated Advenient from other digital marketing companies.

Identity system

The guide includes the approved primary wordmark, single-color and outline treatments, and a stand-alone brand symbol. I developed each to retain the upward exponent gesture while adapting to different backgrounds and applications.

Verdigris teal and warm orange anchor the identity. A wider palette offers contrast and variety where needed, while DM Sans keeps everything legible, from oversized headlines to smaller body text.
